# 9. Future Roadmap: LiuliX Evolution Roadmap

> **Last Updated**: 2026-01-13

We continuously iterate the product, aiming to provide users with a more powerful local data exploration experience. Here is the recent development plan.

---

## Short-term (Q1 2026)

### Enhanced Reporting Capabilities
- **Excel/PDF Export**: Support exporting analysis results and charts to standard formats.
- **More Chart Types**: Add Heatmap, Radar, and Sankey diagrams.

### BYO Key (Bring Your Own Key)
- Allow users to configure their own OpenAI / Anthropic / DeepSeek API Keys in the frontend.
- Remove quota limits of the built-in trial Key.

---

## Mid-term (Q2 2026)

### Data Source Expansion
- **SQLite / DuckDB File Support**: Directly open local database files.
- **PostgreSQL Connector**: Support read-only connection to remote databases for analysis.

### AI Agent Evolution
- **Multi-step Reasoning**: AI can automatically decompose complex tasks and execute cleaning and analysis in steps.
- **Smart Chart Recommendation**: Automatically generate the most suitable charts based on data distribution.

---

## Long-term (2026+)

### Collaboration & Sharing
- **Analysis Flow Sharing**: Package the entire cleaning and analysis steps to share with colleagues.
- **Local Knowledge Base**: Allow RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) to index local documents to assist data analysis.
